    how a cut happens

    1. you drop the files

      A Drive or Dropbox link to the raw recording or the VOD.

    2. we cut it

      Edit, captions, sound and thumbnail, made together so they tell one story.

    3. you leave notes

      On the exact frame. Two rounds included.

    4. it goes live

      Delivered ready to upload, or posted for you.

    fair questions

    What do I send you?

    The raw recording, your channel link, a past video whose feel you like, and the date it needs to go up.

    Can you work from a stream VOD?

    Yes. One VOD becomes a highlight video and a shorts pack from the same session.

    How many revisions?

    Two rounds on every edit, left as notes on the frame. Sample policy, confirm before launch.

    Will you post it too?

    If you want. We schedule and post with titles and captions written for each platform.
