# Cutsway cartoon reel: three storylines (30 s, 9:16, voice: ElevenLabs Chris)

Shared look: hand-drawn cartoon, thick ink outlines that "boil" (redrawn 12x a second), flat bright fills in the
Cutsway palette (Yellow #fddf4b, Red #fb3b27, Cyan #03c0f4, Blue #1c4cdd) on light, warm grounds. Archivo captions.
No client work, no real thumbnails: everything is drawn. Claims only from the site: 12 h shorts, 36 h videos,
48 h podcasts, 8 h thumbnails, two rounds of notes, English / Hindi / Telugu, posting.

---

## A. The Backlog Monster (recommended: a character people remember, and every cut has a cause)

Device: a character does the work. Form: one world (the creator's room and house), object-to-object chain.
The creator's unedited footage is a monster made of tangled tapes and clip cards. It started tiny, after one stream,
and now it follows them everywhere, growing every day. Cutsway's editors (small characters with giant scissors) tame
it: they slice it into a litter of cute, finished things (a video, shorts, thumbnails, posts) that run off to the
creator's phone.
First 2 s: a tiny, cute tape-blob sits on a gaming desk next to "STREAM ENDED"; the voice says "Every creator has one."

| Time | Voiceover | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | "Every creator has one. The backlog." | Tiny tape-blob on the desk blinks. Caption: THE BACKLOG |
| 3-7 | "It starts as one stream. Then it follows you everywhere." | It grows each cut: in bed, at dinner, filling the doorway |
| 7-10 | "It eats your weekends." | It chomps a calendar; Saturday and Sunday disappear |
| 10-13 | "So you call Cutsway." | Creator, buried to the neck, taps a phone; door bursts open |
| 13-18 | "We cut it into a full video, shorts, thumbnails... and posts." | Editors with giant scissors; snip, snip: the monster splits into cute little "pets" |
| 18-23 | "Shorts in twelve hours. Full videos in thirty-six." | The pets race a clock across the room and jump into the phone |
| 23-26 | "Two rounds of notes. English, Hindi or Telugu." | Creator points, a pet gets a tweak; three speech bubbles |
| 26-30 | "Feed us your backlog. Cutsway dot com." | The last pet sits on the desk where the monster was; logo + url |

Sound: playful bouncy beat (pizzicato + kick), cartoon SFX: boing, chomp, snip, zip, pop.

## B. 3 A.M.

Device: a moment in someone's day, life before and after. Form: one continuous take around one desk at night.
A streamer at 3 a.m. after a four-hour stream, the folder of footage piling up into a paper avalanche that buries the
chair. They drag it all into Cutsway; the night outside the window speeds up, the pile is cut down while they sleep,
and morning brings the phone pinging with finished content.
First 2 s: a clock flips to 3:00 AM over a sleepy streamer and a folder labelled "STREAM_VOD (4h 12m)".

| Time | Voiceover |
|---|---|
| 0-3 | "It's 3 a.m. Your stream ended hours ago." |
| 3-6 | "And the footage? Still piling up." |
| 6-9 | "Editing it yourself takes your whole week." |
| 9-12 | "So you drop the whole pile into Cutsway." |
| 12-17 | "We cut the boring bits and build a full video, shorts, thumbnails and posts." |
| 17-22 | "You sleep. Shorts are back in twelve hours, full videos in thirty-six." |
| 22-26 | "Then we post it. Everywhere." |
| 26-30 | "Send us the pile. Cutsway dot com." |

Sound: lo-fi night beat that brightens into an upbeat drop at sunrise.

## C. Two Streamers (the bold one)

Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
Two stick-figure streamers finish the same 4-hour stream at the same second. Left edits alone: coffee cups stack,
hair goes wild, days tick by. Right sends it to Cutsway and goes back to gaming; their phone fills with finished
posts. At the end the left panel collapses and both are on the right side.
First 2 s: two identical rooms, both screens flash "STREAM ENDED · 4:12:00".

| Time | Voiceover |
|---|---|
| 0-3 | "Two streamers. Same four-hour stream." |
| 3-8 | "This one edits it alone. Day one... day two..." |
| 8-12 | "This one sends it to Cutsway." |
| 12-17 | "Full video, shorts, thumbnails and posts, all cut for them." |
| 17-22 | "Shorts in twelve hours. Two rounds of notes. Posted for you." |
| 22-26 | "Guess who's live again tonight?" |
| 26-30 | "Be the one on the right. Cutsway dot com." |

Sound: one beat that plays clean on the right side and slows, out of tune, on the left.
