# Brand

## Name and what we do
Cutsway. An editing and social media agency: gaming videos, YouTube long-form, streams and VODs, podcasts, shorts, thumbnails and posting. Our edge is speed.

## Audience
Gaming YouTubers, streamers and podcasters sitting on hours of raw footage with no time to cut it.

## What we promote
Cutsway's editing services. The reel ends on:
- Site: **cutsway.com**

## The feeling
Bold and urgent. (Chosen by Claude on 2026-10-29; the brief left it open. Bold matches the wide black wordmark; urgent matches speed as the edge.)

## Assets
Colours, typed from `launch/rawpile/shared/brand.css` (the live Raw Pile site):
- Night `#07070a`: ground
- Paper `#f4f2ec`: headlines and main text
- Soft `#c4c4d0`: secondary text
- Yellow `#fddf4b`: first accent
- Red `#fb3b27`: the cut and the burn
- Cyan `#03c0f4`: second accent
- Blue `#1c4cdd`: cool glow
- Spectrum: the logo gradient, `#272ed2` → `#03c0f4` → `#fcf397` → `#fddf4b` → `#fb3b27` → `#761a2b`

Font: Archivo (Google Fonts, variable width 62–125, weight 100–900).

Logo: `assets/brand/logo.svg`, the CUTSWAY wordmark in the spectrum gradient, 8:1. Also `assets/brand/favicon.svg`.

Photo: `assets/mic-2.jpg` (podcast mic, from the Raw Pile site).

Look and story: The Raw Pile (the current version, `launch/rawpile` = `prototypes/rawpile`). Grey ungraded clips tumble in the dark, get razored, the rejects burn, and the keepers become the CUTSWAY logo bar. Do not use `prototypes/rawpile-original` (the old colours) for anything.

## Claims
- Turnaround figures in `launch/rawpile/shared/site-config.js` (shorts 12 h, video 36 h, podcast 48 h) are placeholders until the owner confirms them. Use one in the reel only once confirmed.

## Never
Never slow, floaty or soft. No long fades and nothing drifting aimlessly, because that says "slow" and speed is the brand.
