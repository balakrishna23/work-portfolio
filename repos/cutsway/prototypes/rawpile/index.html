    fair questions

    Who is Cutsway for?

    Gaming YouTubers, streamers and podcasters who would rather record than edit. We cut long-form videos, stream highlights, shorts and reels, and podcast episodes, design the thumbnails, and post it all for you.

    What do I send you?

    The whole raw pile. Recordings, VODs, the clips you are not sure about. Cutting is our job.

    Can you work from a stream VOD?

    Yes. One VOD becomes a highlight video and a shorts pack from the same session.

    How many revisions?

    Two rounds on every edit, left as notes on the exact frame.

    Will you post it for me?

    If you want. We schedule and post with titles and captions written for each platform.

    How do we start?

    Book a call or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us about your channel and what you need, and we'll get back to you within a day.

    Do you work in Hindi and Telugu?

    Yes. We're based in Hyderabad and work in English, Hindi and Telugu, with creators anywhere in India or abroad.
